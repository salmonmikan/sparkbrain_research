## Discovery question

Current v0.5 plasticity iterates `sorted(field.connections)` and stops after
`max_updates_per_step=2000` successful updates. The bounded question was:

> Once the effective plastic-edge count exceeds the fixed cap, can an
> isomorphic relabeling of reservoir numeric IDs change which semantic edges
> receive updates, and is any effect fully reducible to sorted edge-key
> iteration plus the hard cap?

This probes resource/scaling behavior, not learning quality.
