Silent residual state could nevertheless prime or suppress the response to a later weak cue.

**Question.** At an event-queue-empty post-episode anchor, does queue-free residual subthreshold field state causally change the response to a prospectively fixed weak/ambiguous cue, while learned topology, weights, Assembly memory, predictor/action parameters and cue bytes are held identical?

**Provisional hypothesis.** Queue-free residual `potential` and/or `adaptation` can change later cue-evoked spike/cascade/pattern/Assembly response even when there is no spontaneous input-free continuation. Potential-mediated priming and adaptation-mediated suppression should be distinguishable under direct state interventions. This is only a seed; no positive mechanism is asserted.
