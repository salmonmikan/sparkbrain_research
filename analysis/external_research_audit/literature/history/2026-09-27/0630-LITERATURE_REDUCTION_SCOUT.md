### 1. Precise external pre/post timing is established synaptic-plasticity territory

**Classification:** `NOVELTY_REDUCTION`, `DESIGN_PRIMITIVE`.

Bi and Poo (1998) experimentally found that the sign and magnitude of persistent synaptic modification depended on relative pre/post spike timing, with potentiation when postsynaptic spikes followed presynaptic activation and depression in the reverse order within narrow timing windows. Song, Miller and Abbott (2000) then analyzed timing-dependent Hebbian plasticity as a competitive learning mechanism.

**Function supplied:** a well-established timing-local update rule for ordinary exposed synapses.  
**Known limitations:** timing-local modification alone does not establish that a hidden recurrent route fires, returns to visible units, remains stable, or supports a task. Biological STDP results also do not validate a particular software rule or parameterization.

### 2. Delay plus recurrent spiking plus STDP already supports polychronous timing structure

**Classification:** `NOVELTY_REDUCTION`, `DESIGN_PRIMITIVE`.

**Classification:** `DESIGN_PRIMITIVE`, `NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE`.

Gallicchio (2019) showed that reservoir stability conditions based only on the recurrent weight matrix can miss the effect of the driving input, and proposed an empirical echo-state-property index comparing trajectories from different initial states under the same input.

**Function supplied:** an input-conditioned stability diagnostic for recurrent reservoirs.  
**Known limitations:** it is formulated for echo-state networks and does not directly certify a spiking implementation; an empirical stability index is not a task-performance result.
