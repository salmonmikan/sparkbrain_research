## Proposed clue A — Anonymous Multi-Lineage Causal Revision

Question: Can the system maintain multiple anonymous causal histories as overlapping/distributed internal state, and later use delayed evidence to selectively revise only the correct historical lineage, without explicit entity IDs, privileged semantic labels, fixed registers, or answer leakage?

Suggested stressors: variable entity count; entity permutation; long delay; contradictory evidence; changing source reliability; merged/intersecting histories; identical recent surface histories whose remote bindings differ; variable numbers of entities.

Potential discriminator emphasis: selective revision; target impairment minus collateral impairment under intervention; state/memory/operation scaling with problem size; not accuracy alone.

## Proposed reduction floor

Before attributing novelty, test prospectively against strong ordinary alternatives including finite-state trackers, explicit lineage/return-address registers, finite provenance tables, eligibility/provenance traces, fading-memory/reservoir/predictive-state models, Bayesian/HMM-style estimators where appropriate, HTM/temporal-memory baselines where appropriate, ordinary recurrence, local leak/adaptation/refractory plus fixed edge/delay dynamics, and transient synaptic/STP state.

A distributed implementation that behaves like an explicit register is at most a distributed implementation result, not automatically a new cognitive mechanism.

## Proposed clue B — Persistent Uncertainty

Re-examine No-Ignition not as mere abstention but as a possible mechanism for preserving multiple unresolved competing internal states until later evidence selectively resolves them. Compare against confidence-threshold abstention plus explicit memory. If no advantage remains, reduce the claim.

## Proposed clue C — Selective Credit Through History

Investigate whether delayed evidence can return credit to one of several past causal lineages that are indistinguishable from current surface state, without explicit privileged identity/address memory.

This may connect belief revision, continual learning, temporal credit assignment, entity tracking, episodic memory, and causal inference, but that integration is a hypothesis to test, not an accepted theory.
