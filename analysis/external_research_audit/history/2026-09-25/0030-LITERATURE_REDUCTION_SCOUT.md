Yang, Kautz & Hatamizadeh, *Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta Rule* (ICLR 2025; arXiv `2412.06464`) combines adaptive gating for rapid erase with delta-rule updates for precise memory modification inside a recurrent linear-attention state and reports gains on retrieval and long-context tasks.

### 3. September 2026 Kalman Delta Networks add uncertainty-aware revision as a current ordinary baseline

Bui, Huang & Ying, *Kalman Delta Networks: Uncertainty-aware Associative Memory* (arXiv `2609.07816`, submitted 2026-09-07) reformulates recurrent associative memory as a linear-Gaussian state-space model. Residual writes are weighted by Kalman gain derived from accumulated uncertainty and observation reliability; delta-style updates become a special case without covariance tracking. The paper reports efficient scan-compatible approximations and improved controlled pretraining results versus contemporary linear-attention models.
