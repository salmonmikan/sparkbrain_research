The newly exposed drift risk is in the opposite direction: lower-layer architecture triage acquired a positive-signal label whose support rule is weaker than its magnitude-stratified structure suggests.

### `justification_trace`

Formal gates remain well traced to concrete integrity/audit failures. The architecture study's 2.0/1.5 ratio thresholds and total-turnover minimum were prospectively fixed, which protects against moving goalposts, but the current evidence does not show that the particular combination `total n>=20 + equal magnitude votes + no per-magnitude support floor` was independently calibrated as a reliable architecture promotion rule. That part has a weak justification trace and should not silently harden into doctrine.

### `false_positive_control`

FORMAL false-positive control remains strong. The lower-layer false-positive risk is now concrete: a sparse magnitude stratum can contribute a full signal vote. This risk is substantially contained because the result is NON_EVIDENTIARY and the mandatory Analyst stop prevented automatic promotion. A second diagnostic issue is the extremely large output AUC ratios. These may be scientifically real, but ratio inflation from near-zero control denominators must be checked before treating magnitude-level ratios as robust triage evidence. The Utility request created in this run asks for exactly that read-only diagnostic.
