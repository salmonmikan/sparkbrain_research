# v0.6.1 D8 — SparkBrain Mechanistic Theory Revision

## 1. Scope

This document interprets candidate-003 and the development-only D1–D7 diagnostics from the
SparkBrain hypothesis, rather than treating failed test cells as items to tune away.

It does not modify the Primary runtime, reopen candidate-003, change a frozen threshold, or adopt a
comparator mechanism as the new Primary.

## 2. What candidate-003 did not refute

The frozen Primary retained several important capabilities across all 50 worlds:

```text
endogenous origin
same-current-input / different-history dependence
observer and taxonomy non-interference
external-authoritative evidence rules
```

The negative result therefore does not mean that internally generated Spark activity was absent.
It means that internally generated activity did not remain sufficiently organized as a causal,
world-relevant process under all fresh temporal and contingency regimes.

## 3. Mechanistic separation exposed by the failure

The present architecture contains three different anonymous calculations:

```text
A. local temporal transition state
   frequency × lag stability
       -> proposal confidence
       -> shared-root trajectory expression

B. external consistency state
   boundary event × later raw external event
       -> anonymous relation reliability

C. relation re-entry
   anonymous relation reliability
       -> transient proposal
       -> ordinary Field threshold
       -> later Spark
```

These calculations are individually coherent, but their causal direction is incomplete.

```text
A -> Field -> boundary -> world -> B -> C -> Field
```

The missing path is:

```text
B -> future A competition
```

World consistency can change later relation re-entry, but it does not reorganize which anonymous
shared-root temporal trajectory becomes active on the next encounter. Consequently, a temporally
stable but world-irrelevant branch can defeat a more frequently observed world-facing branch.

## 4. Revised central diagnosis

> The current Primary lacks anonymous credit circulation from world-confirmed consequences back to
> the earlier local temporal processes that causally produced those consequences.

This is more specific than saying that SparkBrain is weak to lag dispersion.

The lag-dispersion result is produced by a structural mismatch:

```text
local temporal regularity is evaluated at the transition
world relevance is evaluated later at the boundary
no anonymous causal credit returns from the boundary relation to transition competition
```

The repeated-contingency result exposes the complementary mismatch:

```text
relation storage may be correct
but relation expression may abstain or co-express several links
and the resulting expression still does not retrain earlier trajectory competition
```

## 5. Why simple repairs are rejected

### 5.1 Lowering thresholds

Lowering the Field or relation threshold can convert abstentions into Sparks, but it cannot decide
which coexisting anonymous trajectory is functionally relevant. It risks changing a diagnosis of
missing coordination into uncontrolled activity.

### 5.2 Increasing temporal variance tolerance

Increasing `variance_scale_ms2` can rescue a dispersed main branch, but it is a global preference for
variance tolerance. It does not make external consequences causally responsible for the rescued
trajectory and may preserve irrelevant noisy paths.

### 5.3 Hard winner-take-all at the shared root

A fixed winner rule can suppress dual trajectories, but it assumes that one future must be selected
before the system has evidence that alternatives are invalid. It also converts potentially useful
uncertainty into a designer-imposed singleton.

### 5.4 Selecting the expected world target

Using the evaluator's current target, correct action, reward, semantic role, or expected relation in
the Primary runtime would directly violate the untyped and externally evidenced SparkBrain
boundary.

### 5.5 Copying G3, G4, or G5

The comparators are diagnostic positive controls. Their success shows that explicit transition
abstraction, Assembly identity, or typed functional heads can solve the frozen tasks. Importing
those mechanisms would answer a different research question rather than explain whether anonymous
Field Dynamics can organize function.

## 6. A permissible missing capability: anonymous causal eligibility

The failure points toward a capability, not yet an implementation:

```text
local anonymous event/path activity
    -> bounded causal eligibility trace
    -> anonymous boundary event
    -> raw external consistency or contradiction
    -> externally gated update along the eligible causal lineage
    -> changed future local competition
```

The key term is **causal eligibility**, not reward or correctness.

The update would not say:

```text
this path was good
this was the correct action
this Spark meant food
```

It would record only a structural fact:

```text
activity along this anonymous lineage was followed by a reproducible external relation
```

Contradiction or disappearance of that relation would weaken the same lineage. Internally generated
activity alone could never confirm itself.

## 7. Necessary constraints on any future mechanism

Any future SparkBrain hypothesis that attempts to close the missing path must satisfy all of the
following.

1. **Anonymous:** no semantic label, role, answer, reward, or correct-action field.
2. **Causally local:** only events in the actual lineage may receive external evidence.
3. **External-confirmation-only:** endogenous recurrence alone cannot strengthen a path.
4. **Bidirectionally correctable:** contradiction and contingency reversal must weaken or redirect
   prior influence.
5. **Competition-facing:** the effect must change later trajectory formation, not only an observer
   score or final readout.
6. **Uncertainty-preserving:** several candidates may remain active when external evidence is
   genuinely ambiguous.
7. **Bounded:** eligibility, credit, and generated activity must expire and remain resource-limited.
8. **Observer-independent:** removing all taxonomy and visualization code must preserve normalized
   runtime traces.
9. **Reset/transplant testable:** the new influence must have an explicit falsifiable locus.
10. **Comparator-separable:** success must not be obtained by hiding an explicit sequence table,
    Assembly key, or typed head under a new name.

## 8. Competing mechanistic hypotheses for later research

No hypothesis is adopted here. They are separated so that future experiments can reject them.

### H-A — Causal-lineage eligibility feedback

External consistency modifies only the local transition traces that were causally eligible for the
boundary event.

Predicted signature:

```text
swap causal lineage while holding world consequence fixed
    -> influence follows the lineage

shuffle noncausal transition state
    -> little effect
```

Primary risk: an eligibility table may become another explicit transition-memory system.

### H-B — Field-distributed consequence trace

Boundary/world interaction leaves a decaying distributed Field trace that later biases compatible
activity without directly updating a path table.

Predicted signature:

```text
transplant Field/adaptation/trace state
    -> functional bias transfers

transplant explicit transition state alone
    -> incomplete transfer
```

Primary risk: a hand-authored trace channel may merely rename reward or context.

### H-C — Local competition with externally modulated gain

Existing anonymous relation evidence modulates gain/inhibition among currently active local
candidates without naming a winner.

Predicted signature:

```text
world relation changes
    -> relative shared-root excitability changes
    -> several candidates survive only when evidence remains close
```

Primary risk: direct target-specific gain can become a hidden answer lookup unless constrained to
verified causal lineage.

### H-D — No additional feedback mechanism

The negative result may indicate that the current SparkBrain premise is insufficient: local temporal
statistics plus a Dynamic Field may not autonomously form stable world-relevant trajectory identity.

Predicted signature:

```text
all anonymous/local variants fail
while explicit sequence/Assembly/typed comparators remain robust
```

This is a valid negative-completion path and must remain available.

## 9. Diagnostic discriminators before any new implementation

The next development-only studies should distinguish the hypotheses rather than optimize scores.

### D8-A — Lag-profile permutation

Hold event counts, paths, world consequence, and total lag multiset fixed. Permute only which branch
receives each lag profile.

```text
selection follows lag assignment
    -> current temporal-confidence mechanism is causal

selection remains on world-linked lineage
    -> another state already supplies functional bias
```

### D8-B — Consequence permutation after fixed trajectory learning

Hold local transition state fixed and permute anonymous world consequences during later externally
confirmed episodes.

```text
future trajectory competition unchanged
    -> confirms missing B -> A path
```

### D8-C — Storage/expression cross-transplant

Cross relation storage state, Field state, and local transition state independently.

```text
relation dominance transfers but Spark expression does not
    -> expression bottleneck

expression transfers with Field state
    -> Field-side transient locus
```

### D8-D — Causal-lineage shuffle

Preserve event count, timing, current, relation reliability, and boundary frequency while assigning
external evidence to a matched noncausal lineage.

A valid anonymous credit mechanism must discriminate causal from merely correlated matched activity.

### D8-E — Ambiguity continuation

Present two branches with genuinely equal external support and then allow later evidence to diverge.
The desired observation is not forced early singleton selection, but bounded coexistence followed by
externally driven differentiation.

## 10. Revised claim boundary

The strongest supported description after candidate-003 is:

> SparkBrain v0.6 can generate history-dependent endogenous Field activity and couple it to anonymous
> world interactions, but it does not yet demonstrate an anonymous mechanism by which externally
> stabilized consequences organize future temporal-trajectory competition. Under lag dispersion,
> local temporal stability can dominate world relevance; under repeated contingency changes,
> relation storage and Field expression can diverge. The frozen Primary architecture is therefore
> unsupported as a general causal endogenous model under the candidate-003 scope.

## 11. Relationship to RV01

RV01 remains an independent research line. This diagnosis neither assumes RV01 succeeds nor changes
its design. Later comparison may ask whether RV01 produces a genuinely local and generic credit
circulation mechanism, but candidate-003 must not be retroactively reinterpreted through RV01.
