# SparkBrain v0.6 Claim Boundaries

## Protocol status

Protocol Amendment 001 was adopted on 2026-08-29 before confirmatory v0.6 scientific Gate
evaluation. It re-centres v0.6 on functionally relational endogenous Sparks. Missing-middle remains
one validity assay rather than the project definition.

## Permitted from the implemented foundation

- external and endogenous event provenance is represented explicitly;
- internally generated events do not count as observations;
- positive learning commit requires registered external confirmation;
- an Assembly-free Primary runtime-state schema exists;
- post-hoc observers receive immutable traces;
- observer ON/OFF state equality can be tested;
- G0 queue-drain continuation is currently a negative diagnostic;
- G1 local temporal expectation is implemented;
- G2 sparse local transition adaptation is externally confirmation-gated;
- registered internal proposals can be reinjected as ordinary bounded Field current;
- reinjection does not force a Spark or commit positive learning;
- development checkpoints fail closed on content tampering.

## Evidence levels that must remain separate

### L1 — Endogenous Event

An internally originated Spark occurred. This does not exclude noise, echo, or useless activity.

### L2 — Predictive Endogenous Spark

The endogenous Spark predicted a later Field or external event beyond matched controls.

### L3 — Functionally Relational Endogenous Spark

The endogenous Spark or lineage causally changed later Dynamics, prediction, action, or memory;
formed a stable externally confirmed relation; remained externally correctable; and showed
selective causal impairment under targeted intervention.

L1 must not be reported as L2. L2 must not be reported as L3.

## Not yet permitted

- state-dependent non-copy endogenous cognition;
- autonomous endogenous Spark chains;
- predictive validity beyond queue, echo, frequency, and random controls;
- causal participation in later Dynamics, prediction, action, or memory;
- stable externally confirmed functional relations;
- relation revision after contingency change;
- functional equivalence across physically different trajectories;
- forward missing-middle completion;
- calibrated branching futures;
- a resolved memory locus;
- a causally functional Field-embedded temporal model;
- semantic understanding, concepts, organs, consciousness, AGI, or biological equivalence.

## Maximum future expression

Only after all amended Gates A–H pass may the project use:

> Under controlled pre-semantic conditions, a persistent Dynamic Field generated endogenous Sparks
> not directly supplied by current external input; those Sparks causally participated in subsequent
> internal Dynamics, prediction, action, or memory; and externally confirmed functional relations
> were acquired and remained correctable without explicit Assembly or semantic state in runtime.

A shorter permitted English phrase is:

> Support for causally functional and externally correctable endogenous Spark relations under
> controlled pre-semantic conditions.

## Terms requiring qualification

`functional meaning candidate` may be used only in a post-hoc scientific discussion after L3 is
supported, and only with the following qualification:

> A stable causal relation pattern among internally originated activity, other internal Dynamics,
> prediction, action, memory, external consequence, and correction. It does not establish human,
> subjective, linguistic, or semantic meaning.

## Prohibited descriptions

The following remain prohibited descriptions of a v0.6 result:

- the Spark understood its meaning;
- semantic understanding;
- concept formation;
- imagination or dreaming;
- consciousness or self-consciousness;
- AGI;
- biological-neuron or brain equivalence;
- organ formation;
- proven physical energy efficiency;
- generative Assembly as the Primary runtime mechanism.

## Missing-middle claim boundary

A forward missing-middle result requires:

```text
created_at(C_endogenous) < arrival_at(D_external)
```

A correct missing-middle result supports one predictive-validity assay. It does not, by itself,
establish L3 functional relation, a semantic representation, or the complete v0.6 claim.
