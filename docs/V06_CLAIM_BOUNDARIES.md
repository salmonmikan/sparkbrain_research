# SparkBrain v0.6 Claim Boundaries

## 1. Protocol status

Three amendments are normative:

1. Protocol Amendment 001 moved missing-middle from the programme centre to one validity assay and
   re-centred v0.6 on internally originated Sparks with causal downstream function.
2. Protocol Amendment 002 states that prediction, action, memory, reward, role, and meaning are
   observer projections rather than Primary runtime relation types.
3. Protocol Amendment 003 requires externally learned anonymous relation state to re-enter later
   normal-rule Dynamics, prioritizes persistence-locus analysis, and makes a frozen multi-world,
   multi-seed confirmatory suite mandatory before release.

All were adopted before confirmatory Level-2/Level-3 evaluation. Earlier plans remain in Git history.

## 2. Permitted from the engineering foundation before V06-12 acceptance

- external and endogenous provenance is explicit;
- internal events do not count as observations;
- positive local learning commit requires registered external consistency;
- an Assembly-free and taxonomy-guarded Primary runtime exists;
- observers receive immutable traces;
- G0 queue-drain continuation is a negative diagnostic;
- G1 anonymous local temporal expectation is implemented;
- G2 externally gated sparse local transition adaptation is implemented;
- registered proposals enter the normal Field path through bounded reinjection;
- external reality can match, contradict, expire, and cancel stale branches;
- a single-world non-copy, history-dependent Field-Spark Level-1 engineering candidate exists;
- a single-world autonomous-chain Level-2 engineering candidate exists;
- single-world anonymous boundary/world coupling and relation-revision candidates exist;
- development checkpoints fail closed on content tampering.

The relation-revision candidate does not become a closed Level-3 candidate until the revised relation
state demonstrably changes later Dynamics.

## 3. Evidence levels

### L1 — Endogenous origin

A normally thresholded internal Spark occurred without direct external supply at its target/time.
This does not yet exclude every noise, echo, or engineering shortcut across multiple worlds.

### L2 — Causally participating endogenous Spark

The Spark changes a later anonymous internal state, endogenous event, boundary event, or external
stream under targeted intervention beyond matched random and sham controls.

### L3 — Externally stabilized, revisable, and re-entering anonymous relation

The Spark or lineage participates in an anonymous relation that:

- stabilizes only through external consistency;
- revises when world contingencies change;
- changes later normal-rule Field or boundary Dynamics;
- loses that later effect under relation reset or targeted intervention;
- returns when the old relation is reacquired;
- survives held-out worlds and perturbation seeds;
- remains free of predeclared functional relation types.

L1 must not be reported as L2. L2 must not be reported as L3. A changed reliability counter without a
later causal Dynamics change is not L3.

## 4. Observer terms requiring strict qualification

### Predictive

Permitted only when the endogenous lineage precedes a later event, improves future estimation, and
loses that advantage under causal suppression beyond matched controls.

### Action-related

Permitted only as an observer description of changed anonymous outbound boundary events and changed
world streams. Runtime must not contain an action type or correct-action target.

### Memory-related

Permitted only as an observer description of a delayed persistent effect supported by component
reset, transplant, and matched controls. Runtime must not contain a memory relation type.

### Reward or value

Not permitted for the Primary v0.6 runtime. Reward-driven systems may be reported only as isolated
comparators.

### Functional meaning candidate

May be used only after confirmatory L3, and only as:

> A post-hoc stable causal relation signature among anonymous internal events, state changes,
> boundary events, external events, relation re-entry, and external revision. It does not establish
> human, subjective, linguistic, or semantic meaning.

## 5. Not yet permitted

Until the relevant work packages pass, the following remain prohibited claims:

- confirmatory multi-world L1 or L2;
- confirmatory L3;
- a closed functional endogenous loop;
- distributed Field memory;
- relation generalization across held-out worlds;
- physical-trajectory functional equivalence;
- confirmatory forward missing-middle validity;
- a resolved persistence locus beyond explicit local transition/consistency state;
- superiority over G3/G4/G5 comparators;
- a causally functional general Field-embedded world model;
- semantic understanding, concept formation, autonomous value formation, organs, consciousness, AGI,
  or biological equivalence.

## 6. Required interpretation of persistence-locus results

If reset/transplant shows that G1/local-transition and anonymous consistency state are jointly
necessary and sufficient for the demonstrated behaviour, the project must describe the architecture
as principally:

> A Dynamic Field plus explicit anonymous transition and consistency memory.

It must not relabel those explicit states as emergent distributed Field memory.

If relation state changes but cannot alter later Dynamics, the present Level-3 engineering candidate
must be downgraded to externally revisable relation recording.

## 7. Maximum future expression

Only after amended Gates A–I pass may the project use:

> Under controlled pre-semantic conditions, a persistent Dynamic Field generated endogenous Sparks
> not directly supplied by current input. Without explicit Assembly or predeclared prediction,
> action, memory, reward, role, or meaning types, those Sparks causally altered later anonymous Field
> and boundary states; externally stabilized and revised anonymous relations re-entered later normal-
> rule Dynamics; and the effects reproduced across held-out worlds and perturbation seeds.

Short form:

> Support for taxonomy-independent, causally participating, externally revisable and re-entering
> endogenous Spark relations under controlled pre-semantic conditions.

## 8. Prohibited descriptions

- the Spark understood its meaning;
- the runtime discovered a prediction/action/memory/reward type;
- semantic understanding;
- concept formation;
- autonomous value or goal formation;
- imagination or dreaming;
- consciousness or self-consciousness;
- AGI;
- biological-neuron or brain equivalence;
- organ formation;
- proven physical energy efficiency;
- Assembly or typed function heads as the Primary runtime mechanism.

## 9. Confirmatory claim boundary

No confirmatory positive claim is permitted without:

```text
minimum 5 held-out world families
× minimum 10 perturbation seeds
```

plus no-endogenous, matched-random, readout-only, shuffled-state, G3, G4, and G5 conditions. Results
must be reported per-world, per-seed, in aggregate, and with strongest failures preserved.

## 10. Taxonomy-independence claim boundary

A functional observer view is valid only if renaming, deleting, or permuting evaluator categories
leaves Field trace, queues, boundary events, local updates, RNG state, state hash, and checkpoint
continuation unchanged.

If runtime changes when an evaluator changes what it calls prediction, action, memory, or reward,
Gate A fails.

## 11. Missing-middle claim boundary

A forward missing-middle result requires:

```text
created_at(C_endogenous) < arrival_at(D_external)
```

A correct result supports one temporal validity assay. It does not by itself establish L2, L3,
functional meaning, or the complete v0.6 claim.

## 12. Release boundary

The `v06` PR must remain Draft and unmerged while relation re-entry, persistence-locus analysis,
frozen confirmatory execution, comparator interpretation, or final taxonomy/observer audit remains
unresolved.
