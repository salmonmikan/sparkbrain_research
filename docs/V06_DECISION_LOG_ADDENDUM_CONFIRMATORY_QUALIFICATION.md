## Q-V06-02 — The persistence-locus negative result is frozen as an input to qualification

The demonstrated learned effects follow explicit anonymous G1 local-transition and boundary-
consistency state. Matched Field state alone does not transfer them. Qualification and held-out work
must test that architecture as it exists rather than adding a new state to rescue a stronger Field-
distributed-memory interpretation.

Primary, four controls, and G3/G4/G5 each emit one explicit positive or negative result for every
family, seed, and evidence domain. Missing, duplicate, or unexpected records fail closed.

The first integrated matrix detected that comparator relation-reentry gain used a different
normalization from Primary. The comparator world specification was corrected to exact equality. The
comparison test was not weakened.

```text
3 families × 3 seeds × 8 conditions × 9 domains = 648 records
```

## Q-V06-06 — Strict safety and control gates are necessary for Primary support

Primary success fractions are insufficient. Support also requires:

- null false-positive fraction at or below 0.10;
- minimum targeted-minus-matched chain/boundary effect at or above 0.50;
- taxonomy hash match fraction exactly 1.00;
- zero self-confirmation violations;
- every control engineering contract satisfied;
- complete and internally consistent finite metrics.

## Q-V06-07 — G3 success blocks architectural uniqueness

The isolated generic recurrent/transition comparator passes all development qualification worlds.
Therefore current development results do not show that excitable Field Dynamics are necessary.

## Q-V06-08 — G4 success blocks Assembly-necessity conclusions

The isolated explicit Assembly-conditioned comparator also passes. Current development results do
not show that observer-only Assembly is required for capability or that explicit Assembly is
inferior.

## Q-V06-09 — G5 success demonstrates the strength of human-designed functional decomposition

The isolated typed prediction/action/reward/memory-head comparator with privileged scalar reward
passes. Its success does not count as Primary support; it shows that typed functional structure can
solve the current worlds.

## Q-V06-10 — Development interpretation is capability without uniqueness

The correct qualification interpretation is:

> Primary and all three comparators are supported in the development grid; architectural uniqueness
> is not established.

This is not rewritten as a Primary-only success.

## Q-V06-11 — Qualification readiness and held-out readiness are separate

All eight adapters are ready for the three-family qualification interface. None is marked ready for
the five-family held-out interface until that distinct contract is implemented and reviewed.
Qualification evidence cannot be relabelled as confirmatory evidence.
