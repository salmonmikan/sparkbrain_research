# SparkBrain v0.5 completion report

## Outcome

Completion status: **positive_completion**

v0.5 tested whether repeated unlabeled temporal structure could form reusable and functionally relevant Spark Assemblies. The exact gate outcomes are recorded in `artifacts/v05/reference_results.json` and summarized in `docs/V05_STATUS.md`.

## What may be claimed

Only the gates marked PASS may be described as supported, and only inside the frozen controlled synthetic protocol. Engineering implementation and scientific support remain distinct.

## What may not be claimed

- semantic understanding or grounding;
- human-like concept formation;
- organ formation;
- consciousness or AGI;
- biological equivalence;
- external benchmark superiority;
- physical energy efficiency.

## Reproduction

The experiment is CPU-runnable and local/offline after dependencies are installed. Raw aggregate evidence, report, static visualization, source binding, seeds, and SHA-256 checksums are retained in the repository.
