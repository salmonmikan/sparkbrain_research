# SparkBrain v0.5 completion report

## Outcome

Completion status: **positive_completion**

v0.5 tested whether repeated unlabeled temporal structure could form reusable and functionally relevant Spark Assemblies. The exact retained results are recorded in `artifacts/v05/reference_results.json`, summarized in `artifacts/v05/reference_report.md`, and source-bound in `docs/V05_SOURCE_BINDING.md`.

## Supported result

All preregistered primary gates A–E passed in the frozen controlled synthetic protocol. The strongest permitted phrase is:

> **Causal functional temporal Assembly support under the retained controlled pre-semantic conditions.**

The result includes:

- selective activation above order-shuffle, timing-shuffle, and pure-noise controls;
- held-out reuse under timing jitter and added distractors;
- prediction and action utility in the retained worlds;
- selective causal impairment when the target Assembly path is disabled, beyond matched-random Assembly intervention.

## Negative and diagnostic results

The following are not supported and remain first-class results:

- low-level Field weight/delay plasticity was not shown to be necessary or beneficial over the frozen control;
- one-event omission recovery remained much weaker than jitter and distractor reuse;
- targeted physical-unit ablation produced high collateral damage, so a localized physical substrate for the Assembly is not established;
- the evidence does not establish semantic meaning, human-like concepts, or biological structures.

## What may not be claimed

- semantic understanding or grounding;
- human-like concept formation;
- organ formation;
- consciousness or AGI;
- biological equivalence;
- external benchmark superiority;
- physical energy efficiency.

## Reproduction

The experiment is CPU-runnable and local/offline after dependencies are installed. The checked-in artifacts were generated on GitHub Actions with Python 3.11 and seeds `5501, 5502, 5503, 5504` from source commit `b9668b89d549a9d7379f086661a02c47607c5f52`. Raw aggregate evidence, report, static visualization, source binding, configuration, seeds, and SHA-256 checksums are retained in the repository.
