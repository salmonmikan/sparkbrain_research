# SparkBrain v0.6 Decision Log Addendum
## Development Capability Experiment and Pre-Freeze Interpretation

## DCE-01 — Candidate-002 remains unopened

The development experiment may run real adapters only on explicitly development-only fixtures. Seeds `1000..1009`, candidate generation tokens, and candidate world builders remain prohibited.

```text
candidate-002 capability executions = 0
```

## DCE-02 — Branch presentation order is part of the experimental contract

A path-count vector without a chronological schedule is insufficient. Batched path presentation produced a recency confound in G3 and G5.

All architectures must therefore consume the same deterministic balanced chronological schedule. The schedule hash and implementation become part of the future freeze inventory.

## DCE-03 — The initial G3/G5 chain deficit is invalidated as architectural evidence

Before schedule normalization, G3 and G5 selected the last batched branch. After balanced interleaving, G3, G4, and G5 all selected the most-exposed branch and satisfied targeted/matched intervention contracts.

The earlier deficit is classified as presentation-order bias and cannot support a Field or Assembly advantage claim.

## DCE-04 — Shuffled-relation control tests causal disruption, not forced wrong output

A valid shuffled control need not emit a wrong target. Suppression of an originally observable correct mapping is also a valid disruption.

The control passes only when:

- at least one original relation mapping was observable;
- every such mapping changed or disappeared after shuffling;
- no shuffled response accidentally remained correct.

## DCE-05 — Primary repeated-contingency failure is retained

In the six-phase development world, Primary failed reversal-reacquisition and relation-reentry while G3/G4/G5 passed.

This failure is attributed to history inertia and insufficient anonymous relation support under repeated target cycling. It is not repaired by changing v0.6 decay, priors, margins, phase lengths, or world rules.

Any remedy belongs to a new model/protocol version and a new development programme.

## DCE-06 — Development success does not establish architecture necessity

After fairness repair:

```text
Primary: 34 / 36 domain cells
G3:      36 / 36
G4:      36 / 36
G5:      36 / 36
```

The correct interpretation is capability without uniqueness. Current development tasks do not establish that excitable Field Dynamics, observer-only Assembly, or untyped relations are necessary.

## DCE-07 — Raw resource counts are descriptive, not efficiency-normalized

Primary, G3, G4, G5, and controls count different kinds of events and state. Raw parameter, persistent-state, generated-event, and training-observation totals may be reported, but no cross-architecture efficiency ranking is allowed before a normalized accounting contract is frozen.

## DCE-08 — Real adapter source remains staging-only before review

The real capability adapters and development execution workflow remain on `v06-capability-staging-20260830` for source and interface review. They must not be merged into protected `v06`, marked candidate-ready, or connected to candidate-002 before:

- adapter/fairness review;
- resource normalization decision;
- artifact atomicity review;
- exact inventory and hash freeze;
- execution-seal approval.

## DCE-09 — Negative development evidence cannot tune candidate worlds

The Primary repeated-cycle failure may guide future scientific discussion, but cannot be used to alter candidate-002 worlds, thresholds, schedules, exclusions, or scoring. The candidate programme remains fixed independently of development outcomes once its freeze is approved.
