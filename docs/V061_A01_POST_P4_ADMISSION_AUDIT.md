## Prospective admission standard

`docs/V061_PREMECHANISM_ADMISSION_AND_NEGATIVE_COMPLETION.md` requires a prospective mechanism proposal to bind concrete discriminator/null/carrier content before outcome exposure, including at minimum:

1. causal-lineage swap test;
2. external-confirmation-only positive update;
3. contradiction/reversal correction;
4. future local-competition effect;
5. bounded ambiguity behavior;
6. no forbidden typed/semantic/evaluator-global privilege;
7. expected P3 carrier loci;
8. smallest explicit-memory null;
9. strong generic recurrent null;
10. negative stopping observation.

A qualifying proposal must bind concrete identities for its lineage-swap, contradiction, future-competition, bounded-ambiguity, P3 cross-transplant, explicit-memory null, recurrent/reservoir null, and negative-stopping observation. A changed protocol/null/carrier/declaration is a new proposal generation.
