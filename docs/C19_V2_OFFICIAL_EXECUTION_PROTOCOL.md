## Primary discriminator and metrics

The primary condition is:

`I2_truth_free_symbolic_surface/G1_coalition/E0_global`

The primary metric is Belief-R `BREU=(BU-Acc+BM-Acc)/2`, with BU-Acc on the 1,074 update
pairs and BM-Acc on the remaining 670 maintain pairs. All-pair accuracy and final coverage are
secondary descriptive metrics.

The primary effect is the paired BREU difference between the I2/G1 and I1/G1 conditions.
Uncertainty uses 10,000 paired official-pair bootstrap resamples with fixed bootstrap seed 19901.
The same pair-index resample is used across all five seeds, each seed's BU/BM/BREU is recomputed,
and the five seed BREUs are averaged before forming the primary contrast.

Result classification is frozen before official access:

- `PASS`: primary BREU delta 95% CI lower bound is greater than zero.
- `FAIL`: primary BREU delta 95% CI upper bound is less than or equal to zero.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: the primary BREU delta 95% CI contains zero.
- `INVALID_EVIDENCE`: binding, identity, row inventory, access ordering, raw-before-score, or
  complete official-pair coverage is invalid.

Only the registered primary contrast controls PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE. Other contrasts are
descriptive.
