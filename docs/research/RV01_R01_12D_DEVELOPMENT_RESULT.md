## Interpretation

R01-12D rejects a simple statement that a resource-matched reservoir fully explains away every RV01 development effect: the Field has a repeatable ordered-retention advantage in shared-prefix, reversal, and dense-load worlds under the registered resource contract.

However, R01-12D also rejects a simple statement that RV01 is already architecturally superior:

- exact-route recovery is tied overall at `24 / 60`;
- the reservoir equals the Field on all disjoint and shared-cue worlds;
- reservoir contamination is lower overall (`177` versus `216`);
- the strongest Field advantage is therefore a continuation/coverage advantage, not cleaner or more exact route reconstruction.

The correct development conclusion is **mixed evidence**. A generic recurrent explanation remains viable for part of the observed capability, while the physical Field shows a different retention/coverage trade-off that warrants held-out testing under the already frozen world family definitions.

## Claim boundary

R01-12D does not establish:

It supports only the narrower statement that the current G1/G2-free physical Field exhibits a development-set sequence-retention profile that is not identical to the preregistered resource-matched reservoir profile.
