## Learning goals remain separate

History separation is a prerequisite, not utility: recurrent trajectories can retain
useful temporal information without task-training the recurrent medium
([Maass et al., 2002](https://igi-web.tugraz.at/people/maass/psfiles/130.pdf)). A powerful
readout can also make a representation look informative, motivating matched consumer
capacity and teaching exposure rather than post-hoc probe upgrades
([Hewitt and Liang, 2019](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/)). Recoverability must be
separated from actual behavioral use, with intervention controls for collateral changes
([Elazar et al., 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.10/)).

Retention v5's negative predefined support result remains unchanged: recorded L/Fw and
return L/G spikes, assemblies, counts and predictions tie despite continuous-state changes.
The later 68-call pilot can test bounded inlet sensitivity with crossed-input/sham controls
and native/raw operational adequacy. It does not establish acquisition-time weight-learning
necessity. An F0 arm disabled from identical initialization would be a separately approved
future study of the total effect of acquisition-time weight-write availability, including
its downstream mediation. No new arm, seed, allocation or efficacy claim is added here.
