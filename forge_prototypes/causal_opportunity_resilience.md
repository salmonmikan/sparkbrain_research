## Target capability

measure whether that certificate depends on a single traced influence edge or has
two or more edge-disjoint routes. Return one deterministic minimum edge cut.

time-respecting event graph. It is ordinary graph analysis, not causal inference,
learning, memory, or a novel cognitive mechanism.
